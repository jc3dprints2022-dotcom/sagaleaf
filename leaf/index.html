II · Hardware

Built to be held, not tapped.

  • 3.97″ e-paper, 480 × 800

    Monochrome and reflective, with no glow of its own. Board 1 is unlit; a front light is planned and under evaluation.

    DatasheetDatasheetPlanned

  • Four physical buttons. No touchscreen

    BACK and PREV on the left edge, SELECT and NEXT on the right, near the top where your thumbs rest.

    ConfirmedConfirmed

  • USB-C and microSD

    USB-C on the right side for charging and data. Your books live on a microSD card you supply.

    ConfirmedConfirmed

  • MagSafe-pattern magnetic ring

    A steel and magnet ring inside the rear lets it snap to the back of a phone or a magnetic mount.

    Confirmed

  • About 8.5 mm thin (CAD estimate)

    64.19 × 102.02 × 8.47 mm with the rear skin, about 62 g. Estimated from the CAD model; nothing has been weighed yet.

    EstimatedEstimated

III · The page

Then the device disappears.

  1. Turn. A page turn is a brief flash, the way e-paper refreshes.
  2. Set the type. Fonts, size and margins, with your own fonts from microSD.
  3. Keep a thought. Highlights and notes, saved on the device.

Concept screen: device UI in development. The firmware is based on the open-source CrossPoint e-reader firmware and has not yet run on Leaf hardware. Text: Pride and Prejudice, Jane Austen (public domain).
