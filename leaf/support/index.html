Support

Questions, answered plainly

When does it ship?

We’ll share a date when there is one. No device has been built yet, and we won’t give a date until it is ready.

Is it touchscreen?

No: e-paper with four buttons. BACK and PREV on the left edge, SELECT and NEXT on the right.

Will it work with the SAGA Reader app?

Sync is planned. The app already has a Devices screen built against a simulated Leaf, but sync has not run on Leaf hardware.

How much will it cost?

There is no price yet. The preorder list takes no payment and reserves nothing.

Does it have a front light?

Board 1 is unlit. A front-lit variant is planned and under evaluation.

Where is the USB-C port?

On the right side, below the SELECT and NEXT buttons. It is used for charging and data.

How do I get books onto it?

It is designed for EPUB books stored on a microSD card you supply, and for wireless upload over Wi-Fi. This is in development.

Need the app?

SAGA Reader is live in your browser today. Open SAGA Reader.
