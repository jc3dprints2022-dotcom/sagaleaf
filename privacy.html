SAGA Reader by Proxima: privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. Published by Proxima LLC, Arizona, USA.

In short: the only personal information we collect is the email address you give us to join the updates list, and any feedback you choose to send us. Reading and notes don't need an account. Your books, highlights and notes stay on your device. This website (not the reader app) uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter with no cookies, to count page visits and button clicks. We don't use ads or tracking pixels, and we don't sell anything about you.

1. The waitlist

  • What we collect: your email address and the date and time you signed up. If you send feedback, we also get your message and, only if you add it, your email address. Nothing else.
  • Why: to email you news about SAGA Reader (such as the Android app) and SAGA Leaf. Feedback is used only to improve SAGA and, if you gave an email, to reply to you. We won't use either for anything else.
  • Where it's kept: in Formspree (formspree.io), a form service that stores sign-ups and emails them to Proxima LLC.
  • Who sees it: only Proxima LLC. We don't sell, rent or share it. If we ever use an email service to send the updates, we'll name it here first, and it may only use your address to send our emails.
  • How long: until you ask us to remove it, or until we close the waitlist, whichever comes first.

2. This website

  • No cookies, no advertising or marketing pixels, and no third-party fonts.
  • The landing page and this policy page (and the SAGA Leaf site under /leaf/) use GoatCounter to count visits (and, on the landing page, clicks on the main buttons) as cookieless aggregate counts. It sets no cookies and doesn't build a profile of you. It records the page, the referring site, browser and screen size, and a rough country. It doesn't store your full IP address. The reader app itself has no analytics.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and sign-ups go through Formspree. Both services may keep short technical logs (for example, IP address and time of request) under their own privacy policies to keep their services running and secure. We don't use these to track you.

3. The reader app

  • No account. You can read, highlight and take notes without signing up.
  • On your device. Your book files, highlights, notes and reading progress are stored on your device. We don't receive them.
  • We don't collect your reading data. The app has no analytics or ads and doesn't record what you read. We don't sell reading data, and we have none to sell.
  • Sync is optional. If you choose to turn on sync, the app sends your library data, encrypted, to the sync service address you enter. Book files are only included if you also switch on "Sync book files". Proxima doesn't run a sync service today; if we offer one, we'll update this policy before it launches.
  • DRM. The app opens DRM-free files. It does not remove or work around DRM, and it doesn't report DRM-protected files to anyone.
  • Export and delete. You can export your notes or whole library from the app. Clearing the app's data in your browser or device removes it.

4. Removing your email

Email ex3dprint@mail.com from the address you signed up with and ask to be removed. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm. You can also ask us what we hold about you; the answer will be your email address and sign-up time, or nothing.

5. Children

The waitlist is not meant for children under 13. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we'll delete the address.

6. Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If the change affects how we use waitlist emails, we'll tell people on the list first.

7. Contact

Proxima LLC, Arizona, USA. Email: ex3dprint@mail.com.
