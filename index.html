What it does

Works offline

Open EPUB, PDF and TXT files from your device. Your library is stored on the device, not on our servers.

DRM-free, honestly

It opens books you own as files. If a file is DRM-protected, it tells you so and stops. It never removes or works around DRM.

Notes you keep

Highlight and take notes as you read. Export your notes, or your whole library, whenever you like.

Free, no store

The apps are free in version 1. There is no book store inside the app and nothing to buy.

Why SAGA

You own your books

Most e-book stores sell you a licence locked to their app and their device. SAGA opens DRM-free files that you keep, back up and move wherever you want.

Your reading stays private

Your library, highlights and notes live on your device. We don't build a profile of what you read or sell your data.

Not locked to one device

Your notes export in open formats anytime. If you ever leave SAGA, you take everything with you.

Fair to authors

We're planning a way for authors to sell DRM-free books directly to readers and keep most of the money, not a small cut.

Our vision

Reading should be simple, private and yours. SAGA is building a reader app and a small e-reader, SAGA Leaf, that work together without a store controlling what you can do with the books you paid for. We're a small team in Arizona, and we're building it in the open with feedback from early readers.

Our goals: a great free reader first, then a fair marketplace for independent authors, and then a SAGA Leaf device built to be repairable and to last.
